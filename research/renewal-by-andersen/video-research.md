## Product implications: proposed, not observed platform facts

### Store an approved creative system once

- Product asset library should distinguish product line, window/door type, installed setting, territory, and permitted benefit claims.
- Separate real customer stories, professional talent, company employees, and generated presenters. A generated presenter can explain an offer without being passed off as a real homeowner testimonial.
- Brand video components should include logo position, caption style, lower thirds, approved music direction, end cards, offers, and disclosure treatment.
- Keep market-specific phones, URLs, availability, and offer terms in structured fields rather than loosely mixed into prompts.

### Capture the monthly campaign separately

Client supplies service/product, monthly deal, start/end dates, territory, offer terms, intended creative type, and desired formats. App inherits approved assets and visual rules. It should permit the familiar Zuops-like choice of commercial video, presenter/UGC-style video, or static creative without forcing one fixed campaign bundle.

### Support reusable creative families

1. **Offer first:** product plus monthly promotion.
2. **Objection first:** cost, disruption, uncertainty, or full-home replacement concern.
3. **Product demonstration:** show real mechanisms, material, installed view, or service process.
4. **Customer proof:** approved real testimonial with relevant supporting footage.
5. **Expert/presenter:** approved representative explains benefit and offer.

### Quality gate for the actual videos

When visually reviewed, grade first-seconds clarity; product accuracy; whether people and installed work feel credible; footage/presenter continuity; useful captions; restrained consistent graphics; offer hierarchy; readable end card/disclosures; clean audio; placement-safe composition; and a clear consultation action. These are evaluation criteria, not claims that this agent saw every property in the source videos.

## What would establish actual winners

An advertiser-provided Meta export mapping ad ID and creative to spend, leads, qualified leads, booked appointments, cost per appointment, and attributed revenue over a defined date range. Longevity, view counts, likes, or repeated variants can help prioritize investigation but cannot substitute for that evidence.
