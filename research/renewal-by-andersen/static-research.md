## Monthly offers: real regional differences that the product must preserve

Current indexed advertiser pages show materially different September 2026 offers under the same national brand:

| [Central Virginia](https://windowva.com/locations/central-virginia/) | Buy three, get the second three 40% off + $400 project discount | Minimum six; September 1–30, 2026 |
| [WindowDoor affiliate network](https://www.windowdoor.com/about-us/special-offers/) | $365 off windows, $900 off doors; no interest for three years | Minimum six; additional qualification terms; expires September 30 |
| [Carolinas example](https://rbawindowreplacements.com/) | 25% off entire order + $0 down / 0% interest for 24 months | Separate regional monthly program |

Use these as evidence of offer variability, not preapproved campaign copy. Current search-index text was checked, not every page's live visual design. Details include eligibility, dates, financing qualification and purchase minima; our app should store the client's approved offer as structured content and preserve exact terms.

## Product conclusions supported by this research

1. Client-entered monthly offer should attach to multiple creative types; evidence includes static, carousel and video testing within a single regional lead-generation strategy.
2. National branding and local offers/contact details are separate layers. A brand system alone cannot safely supply the right regional promotion.
3. Store reusable approved creative families and company assets, with monthly offer, expiry, geography, CTA, and destination filled from campaign input.
4. Quality has two dimensions: brand/product accuracy and measurable marketing effectiveness. Use approved samples to judge visual quality; use actual ad/CRM outcomes to determine winners.
5. Future measurement should identify creative, offer, format, region, placement and time window. Campaign-level figures cannot identify the strongest individual static or video.
