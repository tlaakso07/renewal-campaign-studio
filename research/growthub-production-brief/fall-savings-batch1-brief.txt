Voice Direction Packet
1. Voice Role + Archetype
Diane speaks in first person as herself — not a professional narrator, not a salesperson. Female, 60s, warm Kentucky/Midwestern accent, unhurried energy. Reference archetype: the neighbor who tells it straight over coffee.
2. Emotional State + Speaking Style
Primary emotion: relief. Secondary: quiet pride. Pace target: unhurried, conversational, about 2.5 words per second. Never sound scripted, salesy, overly cheerful, rushed, or corporate. Let a natural breath land before S3 ("Then I saw the bill") and after S6 ("Nothing.").
3. Emphasis + Pauses
Emphasize
Never emphasize

Pause rules: Short (0.5s) after "Nothing." in S6. Medium (1s) before the offer line in S8. No long dramatic pause needed elsewhere in this brief.
