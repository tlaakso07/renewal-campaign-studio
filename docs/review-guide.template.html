05 / A clear picture of today

What’s ready. What comes next.

The complete product is still being built. The review lets you try its core creative workflow without pretending every planned integration is live.

Working in the review

  • Private access and saved work: a hosted review copy with password protection and persistent changes.
  • Company-based creation: campaign briefs, real assets, editable static documents and source-footage video.
  • Files you can use: actual PNG/MP4 rendering, earlier versions, downloads and campaign ZIPs.
  • Useful starting material: three layouts, six documents and three written guides.

Present, with limits

  • Home assistant: a deterministic guide, not open-ended AI reasoning.
  • Discovery and remix: implemented, with an initially empty shared library and further walkthrough testing due.
  • Insights and CRM: mapped CSV imports and calculations; actual data is required and advanced reconciliation is unfinished.
  • Community and learning: basic Feed and written lessons; richer interactions, recordings and administration are incomplete.

Still to connect or build

  • AI providers: reasoning, generated images/video, presenters and voices.
  • Read-only Meta: account authorization, imports, sync and connection management.
  • Customer operations: individual hosted accounts, recovery, email delivery, billing and broader support tools.
  • Launch readiness: the remaining 417 source imports, approved font usage, full accessibility/browser review, scaling and operational recovery.

Four different kinds of information

Actual results come from an imported source report and its selected dates, account, currency and attribution. The review has no real results seeded. An empty chart is intentional until real evidence is supplied.

Readiness checks inspect a creative document; they are not performance scores. Numerical forecasts are not available. No verified benchmark dataset or verified winner evidence is seeded.

What stays private

Company uploads, campaign work and reports are private by default. Shared references require explicit publication. A private remix uses the destination company’s media and does not grant access to another company’s original source files.

How this review is different

The email’s private link and password enter one shared creator identity, so reviewers can see that identity’s saved conversations and preferences. Owner/operator controls are restricted. Changes here stay in the separate review copy, not the original local workspace.

A few helpful answers

How do I open this attachment?

Save the HTML file to your computer and open it in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox. Email previews often disable scripts. The guide itself works without an internet connection; visiting the actual app requires one.

Do I need a Vercel account?

No. Open the private review link in the email and enter the separately supplied workspace password. The plain app address and old direct deployment URLs may ask for Vercel access. The review share link is valid for 30 days from September 16, 2026.

Will these guide controls change the app?

No. Feature search, workflow steps, sample zoom and review notes run inside this file. App edits happen only when you open and use the hosted workspace. The HTML does not contain your login password or a private access token.

Why are some areas empty?

There is no fabricated performance data, community activity, verified winner library or recorded training library. Those areas become useful when actual reports, explicitly published references or owned lesson media are added.

Can the app publish ads or run the advertising budget?

No. You can download completed creative files for your own advertising workflow. Direct ad publishing, budget automation and a full CRM are not included by implication. The planned Meta connection is read-only.

Is this only for Renewal by Andersen?

Renewal is the prototype brand because its assets are available. The platform is designed around isolated company workspaces with configurable themes and creative brand systems. Another company is planned as the first live customer.

What happens if I change a monthly offer?

The campaign records a new offer version. Existing creative documents and rendered downloads retain their earlier information until you explicitly apply the new offer and render updated versions. Check the selected output version before using it.

Who owns the work, and is approval required?

The product requirement is that clients own the content they create. Authorized creators can download ready outputs immediately, without an agency approval gate. Rights for uploaded sources and provider-specific generated media still depend on the applicable source/provider permissions.
