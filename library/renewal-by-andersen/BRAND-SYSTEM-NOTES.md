# Renewal brand system — extracted foundations

Source: supplied **RbA Brand Standards copy.pdf**, 145 pages, revision **02/03/2025**. This is the supplied revision, not a claim that it is the latest corporate revision. Notes below come from the PDF page text, not the Drive-generated Gemini summary. The file remains linked in the catalog.

Use Renewal Green at full strength; avoid tints. Orange has a limited role to avoid visual confusion with the parent brand. Rich Black is reserved for large printed black areas, not type.

Pages 12–13 specify the brand's text contrast expectations and approved pairings. Examples include black text on Renewal Green and white text on black or Dark Gray. The document explicitly discourages white type on green and green type on white. Calculate contrast from actual rendered colors when implementing; do not copy the PDF's printed ratios into a validator without recalculation.

## Exact logo treatment — page 14

Use supplied approved artwork, including the registration mark. Do not recreate or alter the logo. The guide says the logo should appear once per visual surface. Further detailed logo pages still need mapping before a production design system is complete.

The inventory includes two directories of primary logo variants plus product, service and endorsement marks. Identical filenames are not proof of identical file contents. Some badge filenames include 2022; they should remain dated source assets rather than default current claims.

## Typography — pages 47–50

- Primary family: **ITC Franklin Gothic Std**.
- Book is the body-copy weight. Medium, Demi and Heavy serve headings/subheads; avoid italic headings.
- Condensed and compressed faces have limited uses. Book Extra Compressed is explicitly discouraged even though related fonts are present in the supplied folder.
- Futura Std is a tertiary family for short uppercase headings in Book, Bold or Extra Bold. No Futura files appeared in the enumerated font folder; do not substitute an unverified font silently.
- Prefer flush-left, ragged-right copy. Centering is limited to special cases.
- The guide gives 120% leading for headings and 140% for body copy. Tracking guidance varies by context; translate the source's design-tool units carefully before creating CSS or renderer values.

**60 supplied font files were cataloged.** Presence in the folder does not mean every weight is recommended by the standards.

## Texture treatment — page 46

Prefer black/gray textures; green sparingly. Keep text and logos clear of texture using a gradient fade. Do not distort or mix textures, use multiple textures per surface, or place text directly over them. The vector library includes multiple light/dark and RGB/CMYK versions.

## Voice — pages 54–57

Confident, approachable, clear and helpful. The four named traits are **simple, leader, trusted, guide**. Explain replacement in language homeowners can understand. Avoid jargon, pushiness and unsupported boasts. The source encourages roughly seventh-grade readability for ordinary content.

The guide includes branded terminology and example claims, but those examples can have dated supporting evidence. Keep the wording and its evidence together rather than treating every historical phrase as a current universal fact.

## Video and audio — page 83

Keep company identity and contact destination visible for as much of the video as practical; the guide specifically makes the persistent identity requirement mandatory for broadcast lead generation. Give the offer strong priority. Choose approachable, confident talent, avoid overcrowded scripts, and align visuals with the voiceover.

Use the same logo, color, type and voice standards as other media. Spaces should look natural and lived-in rather than overly staged. Track available usage information for people and property alongside source assets; this is setup metadata, not an agency approval gate on client downloads.
