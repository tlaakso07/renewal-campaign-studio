03 / Learn by following the steps

Walk through the work.

Choose a workflow and move through its steps. These are instructions inside this guide; they do not change the app or run a render.

Actual saved output, used as a guide example.

What you finish with

Make a static ad

  1. Choose the campaign. Open Campaigns and select the saved awareness exercise. Read its brief and terms before editing.
  2. Open a composition. In Static Studio, open an existing example or start from editorial, showcase or split layout.
  3. Use real company assets. Choose an imported photograph and the mapped official logo. Catalog entries without originals cannot stand in for source media.
  4. Refine the message. Edit the headline, CTA, disclosure or crop. Wait for Saved, then check Exact saved preview for the final wrapping.
  5. Render the saved version. Select Render PNG. Follow its status in Activity & downloads; a failed job should show a recoverable error.
  6. Download or package. Download the ready PNG, or choose exact completed versions in Campaign export. No manager approval is required.

Result: An editable ad and a downloadable PNG.

Build a video

  1. Open the saved walkthrough. In Video & UGC, start with the 15-second or 30-second real-footage example.
  2. Shape the storyboard. Read the working script, scene narration and shot notes. Choose the commercial/product/UGC direction that describes the intended creative.
  3. Edit the scenes. Replace one source, adjust trim/duration or reorder scenes. Review the whole sequence after a timing change.
  4. Review captions and audio. Edit captions directly, or apply one script line per scene. This does not synthesize speech. Use authorized recorded audio where available.
  5. Check the ending and render. Confirm the campaign message and branded end card, save, then render. Watch Activity for the actual result.
  6. Watch before using. Play the completed MP4 to check framing, timing, captions and sound. Download the version you reviewed.

Result: A playable MP4 with source media, captions and a branded ending.

Adapt a reference

  1. Find a starting point. Use a published reference in Winning Ads or an already uploaded reference. The review library starts empty.
  2. Inspect the evidence. Read the usage and evidence labels. A visually appealing reference is not automatically a verified winner.
  3. Set the destination. Open Remix, select your campaign and choose the imported company media that will appear in the new work.
  4. Describe the changes. Choose the composition/narrative elements to adapt and write the hook or direction. Automatic visual analysis is not connected.
  5. Finish in the studio. Create the private document, review its current offer and brand, then edit/render/export through the normal studio flow.

Result: A private creative using your own assets and current campaign information.

Learn from results

  1. Bring an actual ad report. In Creative Insights, load a CSV report you are authorized to use. Meta’s live connection is not available yet.
  2. Map and validate. Match source columns, review row errors and choose the correct report scope before committing.
  3. Match the exact creative. Review the source ad’s association to a saved creative version. A missing match should remain explicit.
  4. Read comparable metrics. Choose compatible dates, currency and attribution. Keep actual results separate from readiness checks and forecasts.
  5. Add business outcomes. If available, import stable CRM lead/job IDs. Inspect qualification, appointments, jobs and unmatched attribution.
  6. Propose one next test. Create a private variation brief using the source evidence and current campaign. The proposed change is a hypothesis, not a promised lift.

Result: A source-backed view of results and a private hypothesis for the next test.

A few helpful answers

How do I open this attachment?

Save the HTML file to your computer and open it in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox. Email previews often disable scripts. The guide itself works without an internet connection; visiting the actual app requires one.

Do I need a Vercel account?

No. Open the private review link in the email and enter the separately supplied workspace password. The plain app address and old direct deployment URLs may ask for Vercel access. The review share link is valid for 30 days from September 16, 2026.

Will these guide controls change the app?

No. Feature search, workflow steps, sample zoom and review notes run inside this file. App edits happen only when you open and use the hosted workspace. The HTML does not contain your login password or a private access token.

Why are some areas empty?

There is no fabricated performance data, community activity, verified winner library or recorded training library. Those areas become useful when actual reports, explicitly published references or owned lesson media are added.

Can the app publish ads or run the advertising budget?

No. You can download completed creative files for your own advertising workflow. Direct ad publishing, budget automation and a full CRM are not included by implication. The planned Meta connection is read-only.

Is this only for Renewal by Andersen?

Renewal is the prototype brand because its assets are available. The platform is designed around isolated company workspaces with configurable themes and creative brand systems. Another company is planned as the first live customer.

What happens if I change a monthly offer?

The campaign records a new offer version. Existing creative documents and rendered downloads retain their earlier information until you explicitly apply the new offer and render updated versions. Check the selected output version before using it.

Who owns the work, and is approval required?

The product requirement is that clients own the content they create. Authorized creators can download ready outputs immediately, without an agency approval gate. Rights for uploaded sources and provider-specific generated media still depend on the applicable source/provider permissions.
